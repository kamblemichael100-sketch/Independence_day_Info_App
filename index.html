📋 Independence Day Traditions

🇮🇳
Flag Hoisting
The Prime Minister hoists the national flag at Red Fort. State governments hoist flags at their capitals.
🎵
National Anthem
"Jana Gana Mana" is sung across the country. It was adopted as the national anthem on January 24, 1950.
🎤
PM's Speech
The Prime Minister addresses the nation, highlighting achievements and future goals. The speech is broadcast on All India Radio and TV.
🎆
Cultural Events
Schools and colleges hold cultural programs, patriotic songs, dances, and plays celebrating India's freedom struggle.
🍬
Sweets & Celebrations
People distribute sweets like ladoos, barfis, and jalebis to celebrate the occasion. Families come together to rejoice.
🎈
Kite Flying
Kite flying is a popular tradition on Independence Day, especially in Delhi. The sky is filled with tricolor kites.
💂
March Past & Parades
Armed forces and school students participate in parades. The President awards medals for bravery and service.
🎨
Tricolor Everywhere
Homes, shops, and offices are decorated with tricolor flags and rangoli. People wear saffron, white, and green.
